# Reason Codes

Reason codes provide deterministic explanations for exceptions and lifecycle actions.

Examples include route not started by threshold, route behind expected progress, low bridge hazard active, restricted road hazard active, no-through-truck hazard active, road closure active, speed advisory active, speed warning active, GPS evidence unavailable, stale evidence, conflicting evidence, stop progress delayed, stop status unresolved, delivery evidence incomplete, human review required, and evidence insufficient.

Generated reason-code evidence is in `generated/supervisor_reason_code_catalog.json`.
