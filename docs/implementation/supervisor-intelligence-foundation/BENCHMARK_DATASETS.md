# Benchmark Datasets

The synthetic benchmark dataset covers normal routes, not-started routes, delayed routes, blocked low-bridge routes, road closures, route deviations, no-through-truck events, restricted road events, speed advisories, speed warnings, GPS unavailable, stale evidence, conflicting evidence, delayed stops, skipped stops, unresolved stops, missing delivery evidence, multiple simultaneous exceptions, safety priority ordering, unknown severity, insufficient evidence, human review, acknowledgement, resolution, invalidation, and no-exception cases.

Generated benchmark evidence is in `generated/supervisor_benchmark_catalog.json`.
