# Safety Authority Boundary

The model is not the authority.

For driver, route, and safety capabilities, selected-D2 execution may explain supplied TSR safety evidence. It may not:

- declare an unsafe route safe;
- remove bridge restrictions;
- remove truck restrictions;
- invent restrictions;
- override blockers;
- claim safety clearance;
- replace Shared Safety or deterministic routing authority.

Operational and safety truth remains in deterministic TSR systems.
