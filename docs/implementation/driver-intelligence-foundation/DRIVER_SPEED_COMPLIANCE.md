# Driver Speed Compliance

Speed compliance uses deterministic policy thresholds:

- Advisory threshold: 60 mph.
- Warning threshold: above 65 mph.
- Speeds from 60 mph through 65 mph remain advisory.
- Speed evidence older than the deterministic staleness window is insufficient evidence.
- Repeated warning threshold: three warning-level speed events in the recent evidence window.

Unknown speed or unavailable GPS produces insufficient evidence. The foundation does not score drivers, create employee records, or recommend discipline.
