# Benchmark Datasets

Synthetic benchmark cases cover clearance pass, low clearance fail-closed, truck restrictions, hazard proximity review, missing vehicle dimensions, residential restrictions, weight limits, no-through-truck behavior, local delivery exception, stale evidence, and alternative comparison.


Additional coverage includes exact-height rejection, safety-buffer rejection, unknown clearance, conflicting clearance, stale clearance, width and length limits, road closure, restricted road class, malformed geometry, missing geometry, intersecting hazards, shorter unsafe candidates, cheaper unsafe candidates, insufficient evidence, and human review.
